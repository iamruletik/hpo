Scroll down and back up. Watch the two coloured bars at the bottom. The green one should stay glued to the bottom of the visible area through the whole URL-bar animation; the orange dvh one snaps late or lags.

Also lift your finger mid-scroll. The green bar should keep tracking during the momentum + chrome settle. That is the delay you were seeing — it comes from the resize event firing sparsely, not from the units.

Numbers in the panel: vv.height is visualViewport.height. offsetTop is how far the visual viewport has been pushed down. lvh/svh/dvh are the computed pixel values of those units right now, read off hidden probe elements.

rAF shows whether the sampling loop is currently running. It should go idle a moment after you stop touching, and wake on the next touch or scroll.
